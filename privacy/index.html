Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Auvoren handles, why it is needed, when it is shared with service providers, and the choices available to you.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for cross-app advertising.

1. Who we are

Auvoren is a productivity, goal-setting, habit, and personal coaching app operated by Xu Wang Zhang, operating Auvoren, in Ontario, Canada. In this policy, “Auvoren,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to that operator.

For privacy questions or requests, email auvoren9@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This policy applies to information handled through the Auvoren mobile app, its supporting cloud services, customer support, and related communications. It does not govern third-party websites or services you visit independently.

3. Information we handle

Information you provide

  • Account information: email address, authentication records, and account identifiers. Your password is processed by our authentication provider; Auvoren does not receive or store it in readable form.
  • Profile and onboarding information: display name or nickname, selected preferences, motivations, focus areas, and answers you choose to provide.
  • Your Auvoren content: goals, tasks, habits, quit goals and reasons, progress, check-ins, journal-style reflections, notes, and related settings.
  • Support and feedback: messages, attachments, contact details, and any other information you intentionally send when asking for help or providing feedback.

Information created when you use Auvoren

  • Progress and app activity: completions, streaks, milestones, reminder settings, feature interactions, and timestamps needed to operate and improve the app.
  • AI interactions: the relevant context sent for a coaching request, generated responses, and limited operational records used to deliver and troubleshoot the feature, if you enable personalized AI.
  • Device and technical information: app version, operating system, device or installation identifiers, push notification token, time zone, language, and diagnostic information such as errors and performance events.
  • Subscription status: product, entitlement, renewal, and transaction status supplied by Apple and our subscription infrastructure. We do not receive your full payment card number.

Information stored on your device

Some app preferences, session information, cached content, and progress data are stored locally on your device. Depending on your device and backup settings, Apple or another backup provider may include local app data in a device backup that you control.

Information we do not seek

Auvoren does not need your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, health records, or payment-card details for its core features unless a future feature clearly asks for access and explains why. Please avoid putting highly sensitive information into goals, reflections, support messages, or AI requests.

4. How we use information

We use information for the purposes described below:

  • create and secure your account;
  • save, sync, and restore your selected content and preferences;
  • provide goals, habits, tasks, progress, reminders, and other app functions;
  • personalize Auvoren based on the motivations and content you choose to provide;
  • provide personalized AI features when you give separate permission;
  • process subscription status and unlock paid features;
  • respond to support requests and feedback;
  • detect abuse, troubleshoot errors, protect users, and maintain security;
  • understand feature performance using limited product events that avoid the text of your goals, notes, reflections, passwords, and authentication tokens; and
  • comply with law and enforce our Terms of Use.

Where consent is the basis for processing, you may withdraw it as described below. Withdrawing consent does not make earlier lawful processing invalid.

5. Personalized Coach and AI Mascot

Auvoren’s personalized Coach, AI Mascot, and some notification features may use artificial intelligence supplied by DeepSeek. During first-account setup, Auvoren presents an explicit agreement that names DeepSeek and describes the information used for these personalized AI features. If you agree, personalized Coach and AI Mascot are available immediately, so you do not need to activate them later in Settings.

What may be sent

When you request or enable personalized AI, Auvoren may send the information relevant to that response, including goals, motivations, tasks, habits, quit reasons, progress, check-ins, and reflection summaries. Auvoren does not intentionally send your password, payment-card information, or authentication token to DeepSeek.

Why it is sent

The information is sent to generate coaching suggestions, summaries, encouragement, and other responses that are more relevant to you. Generated responses can be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable, so you should use your own judgment.

Your control

You can decline the first-use agreement and choose not to continue into the app. After agreeing, you can turn off personalized Coach or AI Mascot independently in Auvoren’s Settings. Turning a feature off stops new AI requests for that feature and turning off Coach personalization may also disable AI-dependent notifications. These choices do not retrieve information already processed for requests you previously authorized.

6. Service providers and when information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share the content of your goals or reflections for third-party advertising. We disclose information only as needed for the purposes in this policy, with your direction or consent, or as required by law.

Provider Purpose and information involved
Supabase Authentication, database, and cloud functions. Processes account identifiers, email, synced app content, and operational records on our behalf.
DeepSeek AI processing. Receives the relevant personal context only when you enable and use personalized AI, as described above.
Expo and Apple Push Notification service App infrastructure and notifications. May process device, installation, push-token, and delivery information needed to send notifications.
RevenueCat and Apple Subscriptions, entitlement verification, purchase processing, and receipts. Auvoren receives subscription status rather than your full payment-card details.

We select service providers to help operate Auvoren and require them to handle information only for authorized service purposes and with appropriate privacy and security protections, subject to applicable contracts and law. Their own privacy notices also explain how they handle information in their respective roles.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with legal process, protect the rights and safety of users or others, investigate fraud or security incidents, enforce agreements, or complete a business reorganization or transfer. If ownership of Auvoren changes, we will require the recipient to handle personal information consistently with this policy and applicable law.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information; withdraw consent; ask how information has been used or disclosed; or challenge our compliance.

  • AI permission: turn personalized Coach and AI Mascot processing off or on independently in Settings.
  • Notifications: change reminder settings in Auvoren and system notification permissions in your device settings.
  • Account information: update available profile information in the app.
  • Account deletion: use the in-app delete-account control. You can also contact us if you cannot access your account.
  • Privacy request: email auvoren9@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

You may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or another applicable regulator, but we encourage you to contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing an active account, maintaining security, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

  • Active accounts: synced account and app content is generally retained while your account is active or until you delete particular content.
  • Account deletion: using the in-app deletion flow removes the account and account-owned cloud records through our deletion process. Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.
  • Local and backup copies: app data remaining on a device can be removed by clearing or deleting the app, subject to platform behaviour. Device backups or exports you control are not deleted by Auvoren when you delete your account.
  • Service-provider records: providers may keep limited security, transaction, or backup records for their own legally permitted retention periods.

When retention is no longer necessary, we delete, anonymize, or otherwise securely dispose of the information.

9. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. These include authenticated access, encrypted network connections, provider security controls, private user-scoped cloud data rules, and limiting access to information needed to operate the service.

No app, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device and account credentials and should contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

10. International processing

Auvoren is operated from Canada, but our service providers may process or store information in the United States and other countries where they or their subprocessors operate. Those countries may have privacy laws that differ from those in your province or country, and information may be accessible to courts, law enforcement, or national-security authorities under local law.

11. Children and teens

Auvoren is not directed to children under 13, and children under 13 may not create or use an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it as appropriate.

If you are 13 through 17, you may use Auvoren only if you are legally able to consent where you live or have permission from a parent or legal guardian where required. We encourage teens to review this policy with a parent or guardian before enabling personalized AI.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Auvoren changes. We will post the revised policy here with a new “Last updated” date and provide additional notice in the app when a change is material or renewed consent is required.
